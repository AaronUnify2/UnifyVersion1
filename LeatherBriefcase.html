Leather Briefcase Pattern

A zip-top business travel briefcase for 2.5 mm outer leather with a bonded 1–1.5 mm leather lining. It has a laptop sleeve, a front document pocket, a luggage-trolley sleeve, twin handles and a shoulder strap. Change any measurement and every pattern piece, seam length and thickness allowance is recalculated. Print the pieces full size on A4 or Letter, or download them as one SVG for a copy shop plotter.

    Print at 100% / Actual size (not “Fit to page”). The first sheet has a 50 mm test square, so measure it before you cut anything. Large pieces are tiled across several sheets with grey overlap strips and + alignment marks. The long straight strips (gusset, zip panel, strap) are quicker to measure and cut with a steel rule, so they're left out by default.
