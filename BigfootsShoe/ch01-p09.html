9. The Meadow Stage

The Secondary and the Animus chose the second door. Upon entering, they were called onto the stage.

The scene that was being performed took place in a meadow with a backdrop of rolling hills. The scenery was so realistic that it was impossible to tell where the stage ended and the backdrop began.
