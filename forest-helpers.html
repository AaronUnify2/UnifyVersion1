Forest Helpers

Five animal friends are building a camp.
Tap the animal whose special skill fits the job!

For grown-ups: this game teaches specialisation — the idea that we each do the job we're best at, and everyone gains. Your child hears each job read aloud (no reading needed), picks a helper, and watches the camp get built one skill at a time. Wrong taps are never punished; they get a friendly hint instead.
🎉 🌲 🎉

The camp is built!

Everybody did the job they are best at.
That's called specialising — and it makes the forest a happy home.

Keyboard: press 1 to 5 to choose a helper, or R to hear the job again.
